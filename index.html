Welcome to Mirch N Spicy

Food that feels
like home.

Our journey began with one simple idea: bring the unforgettable taste of Pakistani street food into a warm, family-friendly dining experience. From our first grill to every plate today, we create more than meals — we create moments.

Signature Chicken Tikka, smoky BBQ rolls, zesty chutneys and rich karahis made us a local favourite. Our menu now brings in burgers, pasta and Chinese-style rice dishes too, each with our unmistakable desi twist.
